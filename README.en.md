## Nice to meet you, I'm Herbs

### What is falavado?

**Falar** (to speak) + **Lavado** (washed)

falavado came out of a dark period. Chaos had taken over my head, and linear thinking kept me stuck in a cycle of sadness. I needed strong voices that understood what I was going through and that I could talk to openly.

I have studied a lot of philosophy, and I am especially fond of the Stoics. That is why falavado started with three figures: **Triboulet**, my favorite questioner; **Marcus Aurelius**, for the Stoic wisdom I admire so much; and **Carl Jung**, the psychiatrist who studied the shadow.

That is where the formula came from: you put your thought on the table and open it up to the guests, as if you were all together at a bar or in a meeting. They talk openly about what you brought, and you get outside points of view that pull you out of linear thinking.

With the table, I learned to understand what my father's absence was doing to me, how my mood affected my daily life, my work and my studies, and what would be healthy to do to get better. I also came to understand my fear of job interviews and why I froze instead of acting to get what I wanted.

And before you ask: yes, always have a psychologist by your side in your daily life. falavado does not replace a real psychologist.

falavado is not only for personal matters, either. It helps with brainstorming, understanding a project, learning how to study and getting the knowledge in books in an easier, more practical way.

I made a point of adding the dyslexia buttons because I, Felipe Bruno Hervé, am dyslexic, and I also have ADHD. Reading has always been hard for me, so falavado always offers to read the answers aloud.

## How to use it

### In the browser

1. Open the site: [https://felipebherve.github.io/falavado/](https://felipebherve.github.io/falavado/).
2. Tap the gold **The Thinkers' Table** plaque.
3. Say what you want to be called, choose who sits at the table and write your thought.
4. The example rounds already come with Daniel and Maria's narration for you to listen to.

The answers are written by the AI you choose, with your own account. Your key stays only in your browser. Your progress stays on your device, and you can keep it all in a file of your own (**Save my MD**) and load it later.

### In Claude

1. Download the skill: [falavado.zip](assets/falavado.zip).
2. On [claude.ai](https://claude.ai), open **Settings**, then **Capabilities**, and look for **Skills**.
3. Tap **Upload skill** and choose `falavado.zip`. Keep the skill turned on.
4. Open a new chat and write: `Para a mesa: your thought here`

## How it was made

falavado was made with **Claude**, Anthropic's AI, and I am very grateful to it for that.

It all started as a first version in a closed, personal chat of mine. I added the thinkers one by one, each with their own sheet of history, ideas and works. Then I ran many table tests, round after round, until I reached the final version you use today.

## Take care

falavado is an AI simulation: these are not the real thinkers and it does not replace therapy. If you are in deep distress, please seek help from a local crisis line. In Brazil, CVV answers for free, 24 hours a day, at 188 or [cvv.org.br](https://cvv.org.br).
